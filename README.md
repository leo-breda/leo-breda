# 👋 Hi, I’m Leonardo Breda

I’m a PhD researcher working at the intersection of applied mathematics and machine learning, with a focus on physics-informed methods for accelerating scientific simulations.

---

## 🔬 What I Do

My research focuses on developing physics-informed machine learning techniques to enable efficient and physically consistent simulations of complex systems. In particular, I work with large-scale scientific models governed by differential equations, with applications to atmospheric chemistry and air-quality modeling.

A central application of my work is chemical transport modeling, where computational cost often limits the scope of analyses such as inverse problems, uncertainty quantification, and scenario studies. By integrating physics-aware machine learning components into existing simulation frameworks, my goal is to reduce computational complexity while preserving the underlying physical structure of the models.

More broadly, my interests include:
- Physics-informed machine learning  
- Surrogate modeling and reduced-order methods  
- Differential equations and scientific computing
